## ❀ 1.4.1 What the Chassis Does ────୨ৎ────────୨ৎ────

The chassis is the structural backbone of Cheese.

It does not directly detect obstacles, calculate steering corrections or drive the wheels, but it determines where every important subsystem is positioned and how securely those components remain aligned while the robot moves.

| Chassis Function | What It Means for Cheese |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Structural support** | Holds the EV3 Brick, motors, sensors and camera system securely. |
| **Motor alignment** | Keeps the drive and steering motors from shifting during movement. |
| **Steering support** | Holds the linkage and front-wheel geometry in the correct position. |
| **Sensor positioning** | Keeps ultrasonic and color sensors at repeatable heights and angles. |
| **Mass distribution** | Determines where the heavier components are located. |
| **Cable organization** | Keeps wiring away from wheels and moving steering parts. |
| **Reproducibility** | Allows another person to understand how the robot is physically arranged. |

The chassis therefore affects both mechanical reliability and software performance.

<p align="center">
  <strong>
    Stable structure → stable components → more repeatable sensing and movement
  </strong>
</p>

## ❀ 1.4.4 Lower EV3 Placement ────୨ৎ────────୨ৎ────

The EV3 Brick is one of the largest and heaviest individual components in Cheese.

In v4, it was placed lower and flatter inside the chassis instead of requiring a larger upper structure around it.

<p align="center">
  <img src="../../v-photos/v4/ev3_low_position_v4.jpg"
       alt="Lower EV3 Brick placement in Cheese v4"
       width="68%">
</p>

<p align="center">
  <i>Current lower EV3 Brick position inside the v4 chassis.</i>
</p>

This change contributed to:

| Change | Mechanical Benefit |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Lower EV3 position** | Reduces unnecessary height in the central structure. |
| **Flatter arrangement** | Uses chassis volume more efficiently. |
| **Improved access** | Makes inspection and troubleshooting easier. |
| **Less upper support** | Reduces structural mass that does not directly contribute to movement. |

## ❀ 1.4.6 Steering Structure Integration ────୨ৎ────────୨ৎ────

The chassis must also maintain the alignment of the front steering mechanism.

Unlike a fixed axle, the steering structure contains moving joints and pivots. The surrounding chassis must therefore be rigid enough to keep these points aligned while still allowing the linkage to move freely.

<p align="center">
  <img src="../../v-photos/v4/ackerman_steering_top_v4.jpg"
       alt="Cheese v4 Ackermann steering structure"
       width="70%">
</p>

<p align="center">
  <i>
    Top view of the current v4 steering assembly and the chassis structure
    supporting the Ackermann linkage.
  </i>
</p>

The steering support was not removed during the v4 weight reduction because it performs a critical mechanical function.

This demonstrates an important design principle:

> **Remove unnecessary structure, not necessary rigidity.**

## ❀ 1.4.14 Mass Reduction ────୨ৎ────────୨ৎ────

One of the clearest results of the chassis redesign was the reduction in total robot mass.

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/V3-≈888_g-FF8FAB?style=for-the-badge">
  <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Reduction-≈222_g-FFD43B?style=for-the-badge">
  <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/V4-666_g-57C785?style=for-the-badge">
</p>

The current v4 robot has a measured mass of **666 g**, compared with approximately **888 g** for v3.

Therefore:

<p align="center">
  <strong>888 g − 666 g ≈ 222 g removed</strong>
</p>

This is approximately a **25% reduction in mass** compared with v3.

The reduction was achieved through multiple changes rather than one single removed component:

| v4 Change | Contribution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Reduced upper structure** | Removed unnecessary structural mass. |
| **Simplified camera support** | Reduced mounting pieces above the chassis. |
| **Lower EV3 arrangement** | Allowed a flatter structure. |
| **Horizontal Large Motor placement** | Improved use of chassis space. |
| **Removal of auxiliary lighting structure** | Reduced hardware, mounts and wiring. |
| **Removal of non-critical structural pieces** | Kept only components with a clear mechanical role. |

The objective was not simply to make Cheese lighter.

The objective was to improve the **mass-to-function ratio** of the chassis: every remaining structural component should justify its presence.

## ❀ 1.4.15 Chassis Engineering Trade-Offs ────୨ৎ────────୨ৎ────

Making a chassis lighter introduces a trade-off.

Removing too little structure leaves unnecessary mass. Removing too much can reduce rigidity and create alignment problems.

| Too Much Structure | Too Little Structure |
| :--- | :--- |
| Additional mass | Flexible chassis |
| More clutter | Motor movement |
| Harder inspection | Steering misalignment |
| More mounting complexity | Sensor angle changes |
| Higher structure | Increased vibration |

The v4 redesign therefore focused on finding a middle point:

> **Light enough to remove unnecessary mass, but rigid enough to preserve critical alignment.**

This principle is especially important around:

- the steering motor,
- Ackermann linkage,
- drive motor,
- rear axle,
- EV3 support,
- and sensor mounts.

## ❀ 1.4.16 Chassis Evolution ────୨ৎ────────୨ৎ────

The chassis evolved through repeated building and testing rather than through one final design.

| Version | Chassis Development |
| :--- | :--- |
| **v1** | Initial compact concept developed digitally. |
| **v2** | First physical build exposed real structural and connection requirements. |
| **v3** | Added reinforcement and a larger camera-support structure, increasing mass and complexity. |
| **v4** | Removed unnecessary upper structure, lowered major components and simplified the overall chassis while preserving critical supports. |

The important lesson from this progression is that **adding structure can solve one problem while creating another**.

v3 gained support, but also gained mass and clutter.

v4 was designed to preserve the useful reinforcement while removing structure that no longer provided enough benefit.

## ✦ Final Chassis Summary ─── ⋆⋅☆⋅⋆ ───

The Cheese v4 chassis is the physical foundation that connects the robot's mechanical, sensing and control systems.

The redesign reduced the robot from approximately **888 g to 666 g**, simplified the upper structure, lowered major components, removed the previous auxiliary lighting structure, and improved access to the drivetrain, steering system, sensors and electronics.

At the same time, the team preserved the structural areas that directly affect performance: steering alignment, motor mounting, wheel support, sensor positioning and controller stability.

<p align="center">
  <strong>Final chassis lesson:</strong><br>
  A stronger design is not the one with the most pieces.
  It is the one where every remaining piece has a clear mechanical purpose.
</p>
