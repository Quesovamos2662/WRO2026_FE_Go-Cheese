This Bill of Materials identifies the components currently used in Cheese v4.
The objective is to make the robot reproducible by documenting not only the
component type, but also its quantity, model, connection and role in the robot.

---

## 5.1.1 Main Components

| Component | Qty. | Model / Reference | Used for | Connection |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **EV3 Intelligent Brick** | 1 | LEGO 45500 | Main controller | Central controller |
| **EV3 Rechargeable Battery** | 1 | LEGO 45501 | Main power supply | EV3 Brick |
| **EV3 Large Servo Motor** | 1 | LEGO 45502 | Rear propulsion | Output B |
| **EV3 Medium Servo Motor** | 1 | LEGO 45503 | Ackermann steering | Output A |
| **EV3 Ultrasonic Sensor** | 3 | LEGO 45504 | Left, right and front distance | Inputs 1–3 |

## 5.1.2 Component Identification

### LEGO EV3 Intelligent Brick — 45500

**Quantity:** 1  
**Function in Cheese:** Main controller.

The EV3 Brick executes the navigation software, reads the EV3 sensors and
controls both motors.

[Component reference — EV3 Intelligent Brick 45500]

**Quantity:** 1

**Quantity:** 1

**Quantity:** 1  
**Controller:** ATmega328P

| Signal | Nano connection |
| :--- | :--- |

---

## 5.1.5 Critical Components

The following components should not be substituted or repositioned without
expecting recalibration:

- EV3 Brick
- Large Motor
- Medium Motor
- three Ultrasonic Sensors
- Color Sensor
- HuskyLens
- Arduino Nano
- front and rear wheel geometry
- Ackermann steering linkage

Small structural components may vary if the same geometry and rigidity are preserved.

## 5.1.7 Final Reproducibility Note

The Bill of Materials should be read together with the wiring, sensor-placement
and build documentation.

Having the same components does not automatically reproduce Cheese.
